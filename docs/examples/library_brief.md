# Fictional tutorial brief: access to library workshops

This is a fictional software test fixture. All context below is invented for
learning; it is not interview evidence, research, or a validated community need.

## Challenge

A fictional neighborhood library wants to understand what makes it difficult
for adults who are new to the area to participate in its free learning workshops.
Frame a useful research challenge without prescribing a service or technology.

## Audience and roles

- Primary audience: adult residents who recently moved to the neighborhood.
- Other stakeholders: workshop facilitators, library staff, and existing attendees.
- Sponsor: the library coordinator.

## Scenario constraints

- Discovery has a four-week planning window.
- Staff can contribute two hours each week to discovery activities.
- The framing should leave multiple possible future responses open.

## Unknowns

We have no attendance data, interviews, photos, or research notes. Awareness,
scheduling, language, and belonging are questions to investigate, not established
causes. Do not invent quotations, attendance rates, or participant experiences.

## Desired framing outcome

Name the audience, distinguish scenario assumptions from evidence, and identify
what must be learned before ideation. No solution is requested.
